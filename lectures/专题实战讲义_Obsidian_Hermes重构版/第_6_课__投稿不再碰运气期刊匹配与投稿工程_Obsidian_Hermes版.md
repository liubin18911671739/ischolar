                  我是某高校的教师

            请推荐 8-10 本候选期刊，每本提供以下信息（用表格呈现）：

             1. 期刊名称（中英文）
             2. 级别（CSSCI / 北核 / SSCI Q 区 / ESCI）
             3. 影响因子或复合影响因子
             4. 审稿周期（初审到录用大约多久）
             5. 该刊最近 2 年发表过的与我选题最相关的文章标题（1-2 篇）
             6. 匹配度评分（1-5 星）及理由
             7. 该刊对AI 使用的政策（如果你知道的话）
            ⚠️ 注意：我会逐一核实你推荐的期刊信息，请确保期刊名称和级别准确。

        🔴 ❌ AI 推荐期刊的常见错误——必须核实：
             1.     期刊已不在 CSSCI 目录——目录每 2 年更新，AI 信息可能过时

             2. ❌ 影响因子不准确——AI 经常编造或混淆影响因子数据
             3. ❌ 审稿周期不准——实际周期差异很大，以官网或同行经验为准
             4. ❌ 编造"最近发表的相关文章"——务必在知网/期刊官网核实

        📄    Dear Editor(s),
             We are pleased to submit our manuscript entitled "Who Is Translating? Tool
             Dependence and Competence Growth Among MTI Students in the Age of AI" for
             consideration for publication in [Journal Name].
             This study investigates how Master of Translation and Interpreting (MTI) students use
             AI translation tools and examines the relationship between usage behaviors and
             translation learning outcomes. Employing an explanatory sequential mixed-methods
             design, we collected data through a questionnaire survey (n = 156) and semi-
             structured interviews (n = 12) with MTI students at a Chinese university.
             Our findings reveal that students demonstrate moderate-to-high levels of AI tool
             usage, primarily for terminology queries and post-editing. While AI tool usage
             correlates positively with self-assessed translation quality, it shows a negative
             correlation with translation strategy awareness. Qualitative analysis further uncovers
             a tension between efficiency gains and competence development.
             We believe this manuscript is well-suited for [Journal Name] because it addresses
             the timely and increasingly important issue of AI integration in translator education,
             aligning with the journal's focus on [journal's scope]. The study provides empirical
             evidence that can inform pedagogical decisions regarding AI tool use in MTI
